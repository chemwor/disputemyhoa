What to Expect: Timeline & Process

From the moment you start your case to final resolution, here's exactly what happens and when.

Immediate Analysis

0-5 minutes

Your documents are uploaded and our AI begins processing immediately. You'll see a progress indicator as we analyze your case.

  • • Document scanning and text extraction
  • • Initial categorization of dispute type
  • • Relevant regulation identification

Interactive Questionnaire

5-15 minutes

Answer personalized questions about your situation. Our smart form adapts based on your dispute type and only asks what's relevant.

  • • Property and HOA details
  • • Timeline and deadlines
  • • Your goals and preferences
  • • Additional context and evidence

Deep Analysis

15-30 minutes

Our AI performs comprehensive analysis, organizing information and creating structured explanations to help you understand your situation.

  • • Document organization and summary
  • • HOA requirement analysis
  • • Response structure planning
  • • Professional template generation

Complete Analysis Ready

Instant delivery

Receive your comprehensive analysis report and professional response templates, ready to review and customize.

  • • Detailed situation summary
  • • Response options and guidance
  • • Multiple response templates
  • • Step-by-step action plan

After You Get Your Analysis

You're in control of how to proceed. We provide the tools and guidance, but the decisions are yours.

Review & Customize

Review your analysis and customize response templates to match your voice and situation.

Send Your Response

Send your professional response to the HOA via email, certified mail, or hand delivery.

Ongoing Access

Continue accessing your educational materials and case information as your situation progresses.
