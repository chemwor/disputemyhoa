Common HOA Violations: The 15 Most Frequent (And How to Avoid Them)

DisputeMyHOA Team

January 2026 • 14 min read

Whether you're new to HOA living or have been in your community for years, understanding the most common HOA violations can save you a lot of headaches—and money.

Every year, millions of homeowners receive violation notices for issues they didn't even know were against the rules. But the good news is that most violations fall into predictable categories, and with a little awareness, you can avoid the vast majority of them.

Why HOAs Cite Violations

HOAs enforce rules for several reasons: property values, aesthetics, neighbor conflicts, legal obligation, and resident complaints. Understanding this context can help you respond more effectively if you receive a violation notice.

1. Parking Violations

The #1 most common HOA violation nationwide. Common issues include: parking in guest-only spots, street parking overnight, commercial vehicles in driveways, too many vehicles for household, and RVs or boats visible.

2. Lawn and Landscaping Issues

The second most common violation. Issues include: grass too tall or brown, weeds in lawn or flower beds, dead or dying plants not replaced, and unapproved plants or trees.

3. Exterior Paint Colors

A frequent surprise for new homeowners making improvements. Always submit for architectural approval before painting and use only colors from your HOA's approved palette.

4. Architectural Changes Without Approval

The violation that can cost thousands to remedy. Common unauthorized changes include: decks, patios, room additions, roof replacement, window or door replacements, and fencing installation.

5. Noise Complaints

Often neighbor-driven and highly subjective. Common issues: dogs barking excessively, loud music or parties, early morning or late night noise, and construction noise outside permitted hours.

6. Trash and Recycling Bins

A surprisingly common citation that's easy to prevent. Issues include: bins visible from street, bins out too early or left out too late, and overfilled or overflowing bins.

7. Holiday Decorations

Seasonal violations that catch many by surprise. Common issues: decorations up too early, decorations left up too long, and displays that don't comply with guidelines.

8. Pet Violations

Emotional issues that can escalate quickly. Common violations: exceeding pet limits, prohibited breeds, dogs off-leash in common areas, and pet waste not picked up.

9. Rental Restrictions

Increasingly common as HOAs limit short-term rentals. Issues include: renting without HOA approval, violating minimum lease terms, and operating Airbnb/VRBO where prohibited.

10. Fence Violations

Common with new construction and replacements. Issues: installing without approval, wrong height/material/style, wrong color or finish, and deteriorating or damaged fences.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most common HOA violation?

Parking violations are the most commonly cited HOA violations nationwide, followed closely by lawn and landscaping issues. These two categories account for over 50% of all violation notices.

Can I be fined for first-time violations?

This depends on your HOA's rules and state law. Many HOAs are required to give a warning before fines for first offenses. However, some serious violations may result in immediate fines.

What if my neighbor has the same violation but wasn't cited?

Selective enforcement can be a valid defense against violations. If you can document that the same rule isn't being applied to others, include this in your response.
