Our analysis helped them draft a response explaining the compliance solution and requesting the notice be formally closed."

Florida
"A homeowner in Florida received a violation notice for a trash bin left visible from the street — but the photo attached to the notice showed a neighbor's property, not theirs. Our analysis helped them draft a response formally disputing the identification and requesting the HOA provide evidence the violation was correctly attributed to their property."

— the dispute letter put the burden of proof back on the HOA before any fine was assessed.
