Common Questions

Is this legal advice?

No. DisputeMyHOA is a self-help document preparation tool. We provide educational information and AI-assisted drafting only. We are not a law firm and do not provide legal advice. For legal advice, consult a licensed attorney in your state.

Will you represent me against my HOA?

No. We do not represent homeowners in any legal capacity. The appeal letter generated by our tool is for you to review, edit, and send yourself. Use of this service does not create an attorney-client relationship.

Do you guarantee my HOA fine will be reversed?

No. Outcomes depend on the specifics of your situation, your HOA's bylaws, applicable state law, and other factors we cannot control. Our tool helps you organize, understand, and draft a response — it does not guarantee any outcome.

When should I consult an attorney?

If your situation involves a lien, foreclosure, court proceedings, or anything beyond responding to a notice, you should consult a licensed attorney in your state. We can help you understand your notice; only an attorney can give you legal advice.
